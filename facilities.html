Facilities

Every Zone, Built for Training

Free Weights Zone

Two full racks, dumbbells to 60 kg, competition bars and calibrated plates. The busiest zone, so the equipment count is matched to peak-hour load.

Cardio Deck

Treadmills, ellipticals, rowers and air bikes with entertainment screens. Booking slots keep the 6 AM rush civilized.

Functional Turf

Sled track, kettlebells, ropes, plyo boxes and open turf for group conditioning and athletic work.

Orientation Corner

A quiet starter zone for new members to learn movements with a coach before joining the main floor.

Yoga & Mobility Room

A separate studio for morning yoga, stretch classes and mobility workshops, with its own sound system.

Steam & Locker Rooms

Individual lockers, changing rooms, showers and a steam room. Towel service at the front desk.

Amenities

The Things You Notice on Day Two

🅿️ Parking

Two-wheelers on level 1, four-wheelers validated in the Orbit Mall basement. The 6 AM crowd never fights for a spot.

🥤 Protein Bar

Shakes, black coffee and egg whites at the entrance counter. Billed to your membership card if you prefer.

🧴 Hygiene

Floor sanitization twice daily, logged at the front desk. Wipes at every station, fresh towels on request.
