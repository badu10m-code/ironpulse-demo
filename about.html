Our Story

This Is Not Your Average Gym

IRONPULSE opened in Kharadi in 2014 with twelve machines, two coaches and a rule that still holds: no member trains unsupervised in their first two weeks. The rule cost us money in the early months. It also built the reputation that fills the floor today.

Twelve years later the flagship runs across two levels with more than 4,200 members trained, and our second branch in Baner opened in 2025 on the same standard. Members get written plans, monthly reviews and coaches who correct form on the floor instead of behind a desk.

  • Induction plan written for every new member
  • Form-first coaching before load
  • Four-week progress reviews, in person
  • Hygiene checks logged twice a day

Founder Message

Why We Built It This Way

"I spent a decade watching people pay for memberships and quietly stop showing up by February. The problem was never motivation. Nobody built them a plan or told them what to do next. So we built a gym where the plan comes first and the machines come second. Twelve years on, our members still show up, and that is the only metric I care about."

What We Stand For

The Four Rules of Our Floor

Form Before Load

Weight goes up only when the movement is right. A coach here will stop your set to fix squat depth, and you will thank them later.

Plans, Not Vibes

Every member trains on a written program with progression built in. Guesswork is the fastest way to waste a year.

Everyone Gets Watched

Beginner or competitive lifter, someone on the floor is checking your last set. That is what the membership pays for.

Results Get Measured

Strength numbers, body metrics, endurance marks. Progress that is not measured is just a feeling, and feelings plateau.
