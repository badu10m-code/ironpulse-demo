About IRONPULSE

A Gym Built Around Systems, Not Machines

Most gyms hand you a keycard and hope you figure it out. IRONPULSE works the other way around. Every member starts with a coach conversation, gets a written plan, and trains inside a program that gets reviewed every four weeks.

Founded by coach Vikram Rathore after twelve years on training floors across Pune, the gym runs on one belief: effort compounds when someone is actually watching your form.

  • Every member gets an induction plan in week one
  • Form corrections on the floor, daily
  • Progress reviews every four weeks with your coach
  • Equipment count matched to peak-hour load, no queues
