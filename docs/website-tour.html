Website Tour: Every Page Explained

Eight pages, each with a job. Open any of them live, or read the screenshots here.

1. Home

The first impression. A full-screen hero with the gym's promise, a scrolling strip of training categories, the program lineup, both branch locations with timings, member testimonials, trainer profiles, a FAQ and the Running Offers section. A yellow trial button stays in reach down the whole page.

2. About

The gym's story: training philosophy, why structured coaching beats aimless workouts, and what a new member's first weeks look like.

3. Programs

Six training tracks: strength, weight loss, cardio, functional, personal coaching and group classes. Each program explains who it is for, how sessions run and what results look like.

4. Trainers

Coach profiles with photos, specialities and experience. This page sells trust: customers pick a gym largely on who will coach them.

5. Facilities

A photo-led walk through the floor: strength zone, cardio deck, functional turf, mobility studio and amenities.

6. Gallery

Real training moments in a clean grid: the social proof page.

7. Contact and Booking

The business page: the trial form (covered in the Booking Flow guide), both branches with maps and timings, and call, WhatsApp and email cards.

8. BMI Calculator

A small interactive tool: height, weight and age give a BMI score with a training suggestion. It keeps visitors on the site and gives coaches a natural conversation opener.

On every page: a WhatsApp button for one-tap chat, the trial button in the navigation and fast loading on mobile data. Try it on your phone.
