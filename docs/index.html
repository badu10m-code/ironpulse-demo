Try it right now (5 minutes)

  1. Open the website and scroll through the homepage.
  2. Go to the Contact page and fill the free-trial form with any name and number.
  3. Watch the confirmation screen appear with your booking ID, then open the yellow My Booking button at the bottom-right.
  4. Open the Admin Panel and see the same booking in the dashboard.
  5. In the Admin Panel, add one offer under Offers, then open the homepage: the Running Offers section is live.
