Demo Documentation / About This Demo

Why This Demo Was Built

A website is a real investment. This demo exists so you can judge the finished product on your own phone before spending anything, instead of deciding from a conversation or a description.

What this demo is

A complete, working gym website. Not a mockup, not a slideshow: a live site with eight pages, a working trial-booking flow with a customer confirmation screen, and a full admin panel where gym staff would manage day-to-day content. It was built with the structure and quality a real gym website needs, then filled with placeholder details so you can see exactly where your own information goes.

What is demo data

These details are placeholders and will be replaced with your gym's real information:

  • Gym name: IRONPULSE is a fictional brand used for the demo design.
  • Addresses: the Kharadi and Baner branches are example locations.
  • Phone and WhatsApp number: a placeholder number currently receives the demo bookings.
  • Reviews, trainers and photos: sample content showing how your real content will look.

What stays exactly the same for your gym

  • The page structure and the professional design quality.
  • The booking flow: form, confirmation with booking ID, My Booking panel, admin panel.
  • The speed: the site is built to load fast on Indian mobile networks, with no heavy scripts and no trackers.
  • The WhatsApp-first approach: one tap puts a customer in your WhatsApp, which is where Indian customers actually talk.

What happens after you approve

One pass of customization: your gym's real name, logo, photos, prices, branch details and contact numbers go in, the admin panel hands over to your staff, and the site goes live on your own domain. Nothing needs to be rebuilt. The demo you are looking at becomes your website.
